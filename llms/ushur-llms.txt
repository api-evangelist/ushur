# Ushur

> Ushur is an Agentic AI Platform for Customer Experience Automation in regulated 
> industries — healthcare, insurance, and financial services. Ushur enables enterprises 
> to build, deploy, and govern AI agent experiences that automate end-to-end customer 
> journeys with compliance, security, and auditability built in.
